The Society of Asian Scientists and Engineers (SASE) is dedicated to the
advancement of Asian heritage scientists and engineers in education and
employment so that they can achieve their full career potential in their
respective careers.

The SASE Purdue Chapter develops its members by covering five key areas:

1. **Career Development** — Offering career workshops to develop the soft skills
  required to be successful in landing jobs and at the workplace.
2. **Bridging Networks** — Connecting students to sponsoring companies; creating
  an environment that encourages the formation of relationships/networks of the
  members within the society.
3. **Academic Preparation** — Solidifying technical skills via mentorships,
  competitions, and academic workshops.
4. **Cultural Awareness** — Providing cultural events to promote and celebrate
  diversity to prepare members for the global business world.
5. **Community Service** — Offering opportunities to contribute to the local
  communities.
